About me

hi, I'm Whitney!

I'm a creative strategist who connects brands to culture.

I started in graphic design and editorial, moved into brand and consumer marketing, and grew into creative direction. Now I'm Creative Strategy Director on the Brand Experience team at Fetch, where I build the pitches, campaign narratives and points of view that help close partnerships with CPG, retail and restaurant brands.

Because I came up as a designer, I bring the strategic brain and the creative eye to the same table. I don't just find the right idea. I know how to bring it to life, and how to make it drive revenue and cultural impact.
